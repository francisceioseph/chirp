Conversations go straight from one device to the other, over the local network or the internet, encrypted end to end with the Signal protocol and post-quantum key agreement. No accounts, no phone numbers, no logs and no telemetry, and no Google services.

<ul>
<li>Works on the local network with no internet at all, and over the internet when you turn it on, with an optional relay you can host yourself</li>
<li>Add a contact by QR code or with an invite link that works once</li>
<li>Delivery and read receipts, typing, replies, edits, reactions, nudges and search</li>
<li>Photos (without location data), files and voice notes, sent in encrypted pieces</li>
<li>Messages wait and go out on their own when the contact shows up</li>
<li>Notifications that can hide the name and the text</li>
<li>The database is encrypted, with its key in the Android Keystore</li>
</ul>

To keep receiving with the app closed, Chirp runs a service with a quiet, permanent notification. You can turn it off.
